Privacy

What Lanterne does, and doesn't do, with your data.

Your backups and key

They stay in your browser. Lanterne does not send or store them. Closing or reloading the tab removes them from the page.

What is stored on your device

Your language, and a copy of the site's files so it works offline. Exports you create are not encrypted: keep them somewhere safe.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the technical data needed (including IP addresses) to deliver and protect pages. Lanterne adds no ads, analytics or tracking cookies.

The diagnostic button

It copies sizes, counters, the step reached and your browser version. Nothing is sent: you choose whether to share it.
