Signal backup on Android: create it and read it

Create a Signal local backup on Android, copy it to a computer and read your messages with the recovery key.

Create the backup

  1. In Signal, open Settings › Backups › On-device backups.
  2. Turn it on and choose the destination folder.
  3. Save the 64-character recovery key somewhere other than the phone. Signal cannot recover it for you.
  4. Create a backup manually, or set a schedule.

Copy it to your computer

Connect the phone by USB (file transfer mode) and copy the whole folder, including files. Without it, messages open but photos don't.

Read it

In Lanterne, choose that folder, paste the key and click "Decrypt and view".

What about the old .backup file?

Files protected by a 30-digit passphrase use the old format. Lanterne doesn't read them; signalbackup-tools does.

Never give your recovery key to anyone who asks for it by message or phone, even if they claim to work for Signal. Together with the backup, it unlocks all your messages.

Menu labels can vary between versions. Reference: Signal support.
