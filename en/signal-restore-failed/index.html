Signal restore failed: what to check

Signal restore failing? Incomplete folder, wrong key, skipped step: what to check, and how to read your messages anyway.

What to check

  • The right folder. Select the whole backup folder, not a file inside it.
  • The right device. The folder must be on the phone you're restoring to. If it doesn't show up, restart the phone.
  • The right moment. Restore is offered during registration. If you skipped it, you generally need to register again. Careful: reinstalling erases the messages on the phone; don't do it if it holds messages you haven't backed up.
  • The right key. A new key doesn't open older backups. Signal cannot recover a lost key.

Read your messages anyway

Keep an untouched copy of the folder, then open it in Lanterne. If it opens, the key is right and the message file is intact. You can read and export your chats, but not put them back into Signal.

Never give your recovery key to anyone who asks for it by message or phone, even if they claim to work for Signal. Together with the backup, it unlocks all your messages.
