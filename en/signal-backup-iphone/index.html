Signal backup on iPhone: create it and read it

Create a Signal local backup on iPhone or iPad, move it to a computer and read your messages.

On-device backups are available on iPhone and iPad since Signal 8.30.

Create the backup

  1. Open Settings › Backups › On-device backups.
  2. Choose a folder.
  3. Save the recovery key in a password manager.

Move it

Find the folder in the Files app, then copy all of it to your computer, for example with a USB drive or AirDrop to a Mac.

Read it

Open Lanterne on the computer, choose the folder that contains files, paste the key and decrypt.

Never give your recovery key to anyone who asks for it by message or phone, even if they claim to work for Signal. Together with the backup, it unlocks all your messages.

Menu labels can vary between versions. Reference: Signal support.
